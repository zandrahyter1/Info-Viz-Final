A visual story about print culture + preservation

We are losing more than readership.

As newspapers and magazines move toward digital formats, what happens to the physical publications that hold a record of how we saw, made, and understood the world?

01 / CONTEXT

Our relationship with print is changing.

More reading and advertising activity is moving online. But a publication is not only the information printed on its pages. It is also an object: designed, handled, collected, and kept.

This story looks at the pressure on print, then asks what is lost when preservation focuses only on digital access.

04 / THE TURN

The information can survive. The object is harder to replace.

A digital scan can preserve words and images. It cannot fully preserve the weight of the paper, the scale of a page, its folds, binding, printing process, or the way the object was used.

Physical

Evidence in the material itself.

WHAT THE ORIGINAL CAN PRESERVEPaper weight and texture, binding, dimensions, folds, printing methods, annotations, and signs of use. These physical details can carry historical and cultural meaning beyond the text.
05 / SUPPORTING EVIDENCE

Paper can shape how we understand what we read.

A meta-analysis of 54 studies found a small average advantage for reading comprehension on paper compared with digital screens.

A small average advantage for print
Pooled standardized effect size (Hedges’ g)

Hover, focus, or tap the pink data point to learn what the result means.

Hedges’ g = +0.21 · Across 54 studies, the pooled effect favored reading comprehension on paper by a small standardized amount. This does not mean comprehension improved by 21%, and it does not measure every reader’s individual performance.
Source: Delgado et al. (2018), “Don't throw away your printed books.”
06 / THE ASK

Preserve the object, not just the information.

Libraries, archives, and cultural institutions should preserve physical publications alongside their digital counterparts, so future generations can study the full cultural record.
